Who we are

A jemea built on knowledge & service

Ye Birhan Dana is a community of EIADE students learning comparative religion and turning that knowledge into da'wah, education and care for the ummah.

01

Our Mission

To equip Muslims of every age with sound knowledge and confident answers, and to share the message of Islam with wisdom.

02

Our Vision

A generation that knows its deen deeply, understands other worldviews fairly, and lights the path for others.

03

Our Values

Sincerity, knowledge, wisdom and service — guided by the Qur'an and Sunnah.

What we do

Programs & services

From classrooms to stages to screens, we serve wherever the ummah needs us.

Summer & Winter College Student Trainings

Intensive seasonal trainings for university students on comparative religion, logic, atheism and answering doubts — built to prepare confident, well-grounded young Muslims.
